How it works

No accounts, no phone numbers, no pairing codes. Connect in three simple steps.

Step 1: Connect both devices to the same local network

Your PC and mobile device must share the same local network path. Choose any method below:

WiFi Router Both devices connected to same home or office WiFi
Mobile Hotspot Turn on phone hotspot, connect PC to it (No data pack needed)
USB Tethering Connect phone to PC with USB cable and enable USB Tethering
1

Launch ADropX on PC

Open ADropX on your computer. It launches in 0.05 seconds and displays your unique connection QR code.

2

Scan QR Code

Open your phone camera or the ADropX companion app, point at the screen, and connect in one second.

3

Transfer Instantly

Drag and drop files, sync clipboard text, or stream 4K videos. Everything transfers at maximum hardware speed.
